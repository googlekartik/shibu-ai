Sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. Without it, you can still chat — your history just stays in this browser and on this server session, and is not saved to a database.

With sign-in, Shibu AI can:

  • Save your chats under your Google account (when DynamoDB is enabled)
  • Remember your tone / level preferences
  • Let you export or delete all of your saved data

Shibu AI stores only your Google account ID, display name and email in its session. It never stores your Google password or Google tokens, and it never uses your email as your account key — your stable Google ID is the key.
